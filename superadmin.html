Per-SKU product links for the in-camera shop card. Shopee/Lazada: paste the URL and enter image URL + price manually. Shopify: paste URL + Auto-fill. Active when the “Shop the look” feature is on.

Attract / idle mirror — tuning

Active when the “Attract / idle mirror” feature is on. After the idle delay with no interaction, the kiosk auto-starts a live camera teaser (needs camera permission already granted).

QR deep link — scan to phone

The QR encodes a location-tagged link. Phones can't reach a tailnet address, so the base URL must be your PUBLIC (GitHub Pages) URL. Scan-ping URL is the optional Tailscale Funnel endpoint that logs real scans.

Live backend — real-time sync & fleet
